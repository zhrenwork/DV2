6

CONCLUSION

Key takeaways from the data story.

⌄

Key takeaways

  • Food spending is stable and forms the largest share of the weekly household budget.
  • Leisure spending is flexible but important for family wellbeing.
  • Travel spending is occasional and seasonal, creating larger budget peaks.
  • Seasonality plays a big role: summer drives leisure and travel.

Family spending journey

Connected dots summarise how key categories have moved over time.

The connected dots summarise the story: food remains steady, leisure grows gradually, and travel creates the largest change over time.

⭐ Overall message

Melbourne families allocate significant portions of their budget to lifestyle and experiences. As income rises and travel recovers, thoughtful planning can help balance essentials with meaningful life experiences.
